I am a Research Assistant at the Cognitive Agents and Interaction Lab (CAIL), University of Dhaka, advised by Dr. Md Mosaddek Khan. I received my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from North South University in 2025.

My research interests include automata theory and language model reasoning, with a focus on synthesizing and behaviorally verifying computational models from formal language specifications.
