S. M. Muhtasimul Hasan

I am S. M. Muhtasimul Hasan, a Research Assistant at the Cognitive Agents and Interaction Lab (CAIL), University of Dhaka, where I work under the supervision of Dr. Md Mosaddek Khan. My research focuses on the reasoning and reliability of language models, with an emphasis on their ability to construct formal artifacts from structured specifications. I draw on ideas from automata theory to ground LLM reasoning in settings that establish principled connections between linguistic specification, symbolic representation, and executable computation, enabling model reasoning to be studied through behaviorally verifiable mechanisms. Additionally, I study multimodal reasoning and representation learning across vision, language and tabular modalities.
